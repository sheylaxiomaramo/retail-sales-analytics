# Retail Sales Analytics

## Overview

Retail Sales Analytics is a Data Analytics project focused on evaluating the commercial performance of four retail stores using sales, customer satisfaction, product categories, and shipping cost indicators.

The project aims to identify business patterns, compare store performance, and generate data-driven recommendations to support strategic decision-making.

---

# Business Problem

Retail companies need to understand store performance to improve sales strategies, customer experience, and operational efficiency.

This project analyzes multiple business indicators to identify:
- Top-performing stores
- Customer satisfaction trends
- Most demanded product categories
- Best-selling products
- Shipping cost efficiency

---

# Project Objectives

## General Objective

Analyze the commercial performance of Alura Store LATAM through exploratory data analysis and business intelligence techniques.

## Specific Objectives

- Compare total revenue between stores
- Identify the most sold product categories
- Analyze customer satisfaction through ratings
- Detect best-selling and low-rotation products
- Evaluate average shipping costs
- Generate strategic business recommendations

# Main Insights

## Revenue Analysis

Store 1 generated the highest total revenue, demonstrating the strongest commercial performance among all stores.

## Customer Satisfaction

Store 3 achieved the highest average customer rating, indicating better customer perception and experience.

## Product Categories

Furniture was one of the most demanded categories across multiple stores.

## Shipping Costs

Store 4 presented the lowest average shipping cost, representing a competitive operational advantage.

---

# Strategic Recommendation

Based on the analysis, Store 1 is recommended as the best option when prioritizing revenue generation.

However, Store 3 also represents a strong alternative due to its high customer satisfaction and balanced commercial performance.
